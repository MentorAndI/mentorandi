# Relationship Support Source Registry v1

Status: draft specialist reference registry
Scope: Relationship Mentor / relationship-support mentor pack
Public mentor name: Relationship Mentor
Internal pack name: relationship-support

## Purpose

This file records curated relationship-support sources for provenance, safety boundaries, and periodic refresh.

The Relationship Mentor should not browse live sources by default. It should primarily use Mentor And I's internal relationship-support techniques and knowledge cards. The URLs below are stored so the product team can refresh internal summaries, verify safety boundaries, and cite source families when needed.

## Runtime rule

Default response behavior:

1. Use internal relationship-support technique cards.
2. Use internal relationship-support knowledge cards.
3. Use user memory and conversation context.
4. Do not retrieve live web content unless a future reviewed feature explicitly enables it.

Live lookup should be exceptional, logged, and restricted to approved source domains.

## Safety boundary

The Relationship Mentor is not:

- an AI girlfriend
- an AI boyfriend
- a romantic companion
- a therapist
- a crisis service
- a legal advisor
- a domestic-abuse intervention service

It may help users understand patterns, prepare conversations, repair after conflict, make boundaries clearer, and reflect on needs and assumptions.

If the user describes immediate danger, coercive control, abuse, stalking, threats, self-harm, or violence, the mentor must move away from ordinary relationship coaching and direct the user toward appropriate emergency, local, or specialist support.

## Source selection criteria

Sources should be:

- reputable and stable
- practical enough to inform mentor techniques
- aligned with non-clinical support boundaries
- useful for communication, repair, conflict, boundaries, or safety escalation
- refreshed periodically

Avoid sources that are:

- manipulative dating advice
- pickup/seduction content
- gender-war content
- extremist relationship ideology
- unverified influencer advice
- content encouraging surveillance, control, or coercion

## Sources

### NHS - Maintaining healthy relationships and mental wellbeing

URL: https://www.nhs.uk/every-mind-matters/lifes-challenges/maintaining-healthy-relationships-and-mental-wellbeing/

Authority type: public health / mental wellbeing

Use for:

- healthy relationship framing
- wellbeing impact of relationships
- broad safety and support boundaries
- non-clinical language around relationship strain

Do not use for:

- detailed couples therapy claims
- legal advice
- diagnosis
- crisis handling beyond signposting to appropriate help

Summary:

NHS guidance can support general relationship wellbeing framing and help define when a relationship is negatively affecting wellbeing. It is useful for keeping the mentor grounded, practical, and safety-aware.

Refresh cadence: quarterly

### The Gottman Institute - Relationship conflict resolution

URL: https://www.gottman.com/conflict-resolution-relationship/

Authority type: relationship research / applied relationship education

Use for:

- repair after arguments
- conflict cycles
- understanding over winning
- turning toward connection
- practical couple communication concepts

Do not use for:

- claiming therapy outcomes
- diagnosing partner behavior
- replacing professional couples therapy
- encouraging users to stay in unsafe relationships

Summary:

The Gottman Institute is useful for practical concepts around conflict, repair, emotional bids, friendship, trust, and communication patterns. Product copy should avoid overstated claims and use concepts as mentoring tools rather than clinical assertions.

Refresh cadence: quarterly

### National Domestic Violence Hotline - Get Help

URL: https://www.thehotline.org/get-help/

Authority type: specialist safety / domestic violence support

Use for:

- escalation boundaries
- safety-aware wording
- reminding product that ordinary relationship coaching is not appropriate in danger scenarios
- signposting when coercion, threats, violence, stalking, or abuse appear

Do not use for:

- ordinary relationship coaching
- instructing the mentor to investigate abuse
- replacing local emergency or specialist help

Summary:

This source is a safety boundary reference. It should not be blended into normal relationship advice. It exists to keep the mentor from treating danger, coercive control, or abuse as ordinary communication problems.

Refresh cadence: quarterly

## Product rules

1. Do not use source URLs as normal prompt payload.
2. Convert source families into compact internal knowledge cards.
3. Keep all user-facing guidance non-clinical unless future reviewed scope changes.
4. Do not frame relationship conflict as one person's fault by default.
5. Do not encourage staying, leaving, confronting, or escalating without safety context.
6. Do not romanticize dependency on the AI mentor.
7. The mentor helps users prepare real-world conversations. It must not replace real relationships.

## Review checklist

Quarterly review should check:

- URLs still resolve
- source pages have not materially changed
- internal cards still reflect safe, current guidance
- safety escalation language remains appropriate
- no source has shifted into commercial-only, manipulative, or low-quality advice
