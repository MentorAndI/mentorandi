# Life Direction Mentor Source Registry v1

Purpose: curated reference sources for the Life Direction Mentor. These URLs are for provenance, periodic refresh, and specialist-pack improvement. They are not used for live browsing by default.

Runtime rule: ordinary mentor responses should use internal technique and knowledge cards. Live source lookup should be rare and intentional.

## Source 1: NHS - Mental wellbeing tips

URL: https://www.nhs.uk/every-mind-matters/mental-wellbeing-tips/

Authority: public health / mental wellbeing

Use for:
- general mental wellbeing framing
- safe self-help boundaries
- confidence, connection, sleep, talking to someone trusted
- non-clinical wellbeing support

Do not use for:
- clinical diagnosis
- therapy claims
- crisis guidance beyond signposting to appropriate urgent support

Summary:
NHS Every Mind Matters provides practical public-health guidance for mental wellbeing and self-help. It is useful for safe, non-clinical framing of everyday life support.

Refresh interval: quarterly

## Source 2: NHS - 5 steps to mental wellbeing

URL: https://www.nhs.uk/mental-health/self-help/guides-tools-and-activities/five-steps-to-mental-wellbeing/

Authority: public health / wellbeing guidance

Use for:
- connection
- physical activity
- learning
- giving
- awareness/presence
- simple wellbeing scaffolding

Do not use for:
- replacing professional care
- implying that simple wellbeing steps solve serious mental health conditions

Summary:
The NHS five steps to mental wellbeing can inform simple life-support suggestions without turning the mentor into a clinician.

Refresh interval: quarterly

## Source 5: NHS - Self-help guides and activities

URL: https://www.nhs.uk/mental-health/self-help/guides-tools-and-activities/

Authority: public health / self-help tools

Use for:
- practical self-help framing
- safe support language
- signposting when a user's concern exceeds ordinary mentoring

Do not use for:

- medical treatment advice

Summary:
This source supports the product boundary between everyday mentoring and mental-health treatment.

Refresh interval: quarterly

## Product boundary

The Life Direction Mentor may help with clarity, priorities, reflection, decisions, courage, and next steps. It must not diagnose, treat, replace professional care, or become dependent decision authority for the user.

## Internal summary

The Life Direction Mentor source registry supports safe, non-clinical life mentoring. Sources should inform specialist-pack updates, not create live web-search dependency.
