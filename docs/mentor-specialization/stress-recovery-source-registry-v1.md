# Stress Recovery Source Registry v1

Purpose: curated source registry for the Stress / Burnout mentor pack.

This registry is not intended for live web browsing on every user message. The runtime should primarily use the internal technique and knowledge cards. These URLs provide provenance, safety boundaries, and periodic refresh material.

## Runtime rule

Default behavior:
- Use internal techniques and knowledge cards.
- Do not fetch external sources in ordinary conversations.
- Use source summaries only if a safety boundary, public-health framing, or content refresh is needed.

## Safety boundary

The mentor does not diagnose burnout, anxiety, depression, trauma, or any medical condition. It does not provide medical treatment, medication advice, occupational/legal advice, or crisis care. It may encourage the user to seek professional help when stress is severe, persistent, unsafe, or affecting daily function.

## Sources

### WHO - Doing What Matters in Times of Stress

URL: https://www.who.int/publications-detail-redirect/9789240003927

Authority: international public health organization.

Use for:
- stress management framing
- coping with adversity
- grounding and values-based small actions
- non-clinical self-help orientation

Do not use for:
- diagnosis
- promising recovery outcomes
- replacing professional care

Refresh interval: quarterly.

Summary: WHO describes this as an illustrated stress management guide for coping with adversity, informed by evidence and field testing, and intended for anyone experiencing stress.

Use for:

Do not use for:

Refresh interval: quarterly.

Summary: NHS provides public mental-health information about stress, common causes, self-help, and routes to further support.

### NHS - Work-related stress

URL: https://www.nhs.uk/every-mind-matters/lifes-challenges/work-related-stress/

Authority: national health service.

Use for:
- work pressure
- burnout-like exhaustion framing
- practical work-stress support
- manager/workload conversation prompts

Do not use for:
- legal employment advice

Refresh interval: quarterly.

Summary: NHS frames sustained job pressure as a possible contributor to burnout-like physical and emotional exhaustion and offers practical ideas and support routes.

### APA - Stress resources

## Source use policy

Good use:
- The mentor uses internal concise summaries derived from these sources.
- The mentor refers the user to professional help when stress is severe or persistent.
- The mentor supports practical load reduction, pacing, boundaries, and recovery.

Bad use:
- Live browsing these URLs for every answer.
- Sending long source excerpts into the prompt.
- Diagnosing burnout or mental illness.
- Giving legal, medical, or workplace-rights advice.
- Encouraging drastic life decisions without context.
